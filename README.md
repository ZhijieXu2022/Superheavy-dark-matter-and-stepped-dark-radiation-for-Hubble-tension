# Superheavy dark matter and stepped dark radiation for Hubble Tension

Xu Z., 2026, *Superheavy dark matter and stepped dark radiation for Hubble Tension*, Research Square preprint, [doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-8570078/v3](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-8570078/v3). The earlier data record of this work is [zenodo.org/records/23001771](https://zenodo.org/records/23001771).

## Major findings

- The free-streaming mass equals the particle mass at m_Xc = 1.6×10⁻¹⁵ kg = 0.9×10¹² GeV.
- A two-body gravitational bound state of 10¹² GeV particles has a size of 1.2×10⁻¹³ m, an internal speed of 5×10⁻⁷ m/s and forms at t_X = 5×10⁻⁷ s (T ≈ 1 GeV).
- The gravity-induced bulk velocity of dark matter is frozen during radiation domination at 8–9 km/s.
- Cold freeze-out leaves a relic fraction γ ≈ 10⁻⁹ (8×10⁻¹⁰ for 10¹² GeV); ΔN_eff = 0.4 needs a dark-radiation share f_DR ≈ 0.2 of the converted energy.
- In the stepped dark-radiation embedding, the measured drag R_Γ = 0.07 gives a DM–DR Yukawa coupling λ_X ≈ 8×10⁻⁵; a Planck-suppressed portal gives y_K ≈ 7×10⁻¹⁵ and a no-scale supergravity scalar mass of about 0.5 eV.
- Gravitational production with γ ≈ 10⁻⁸ needs H_I ≈ 10¹² GeV, T_RH ≈ 10¹⁵ GeV and a tensor-to-scalar ratio r ≈ 2×10⁻⁵.
